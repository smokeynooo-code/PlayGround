HOW TO MOVE

W A S D to walk · SHIFT to run · Drag the world to look around · E to interact. On touch screens, drag anywhere in the open world and use the on-screen controls. K mutes the city.

Buildings, parked cars, market furniture and burning bins are solid. Enter lit doorways to find crowded rooms and people at work. Get close to a cat and press E to pet it. Birds peck and scatter. The − on the field map shrinks it, and M collapses the directive.

Each tier is sealed by a great gate. Wake its relay, then pull the gate lever beside it to raise the doors. At the perimeter, a final lever wakes only after all three relays are online.
